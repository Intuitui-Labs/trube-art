## 🎨 Architectural Primitives

The engine organizes living visual art into three composable layers:

1. **`surface`**: Atmospheric light behavior and GPU field illumination.
2. **`ornament`**: Micro-detail, overtone harmonics, and procedural textures.
3. **`motif`**: Hand-crafted symbolic line geometry (such as lotus, branch, or tidal forms).
